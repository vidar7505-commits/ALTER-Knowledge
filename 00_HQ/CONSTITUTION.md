# A L T E R . Constitution

## 1. Purpose

Build a consumer fashion product people choose to use repeatedly, while developing a defensible behavioral understanding of how products are discovered, desired, selected, combined, used, maintained, shared, and abandoned.

## 2. Product thesis

A L T E R . must create direct user value before its data can create business value.

The user product is not a survey panel or research interface. It is a high-quality discovery, identity, styling, ownership, and community experience. Behavioral data is generated as a consequence of genuine use.

## 3. Company thesis

A L T E R . may evolve into consumer decision and product-life intelligence infrastructure. This remains a hypothesis until real product usage and external outcomes support it.

## 4. Decision hierarchy

Decisions are ordered by the following evidence hierarchy:

1. Repeated real-user behavior
2. Real economic or consequential choices
3. Controlled product experiments
4. High-quality independent research
5. Expert judgment
6. Founder intuition

Intuition may initiate a test but may not be presented as evidence.

## 5. Execution principles

- Ship learning-capable releases rather than complete visions.
- Preserve raw behavioral data and version experiment definitions.
- Prefer reversible decisions and short feedback loops.
- Do not delay a current release for speculative optimization.
- Professional experience quality is part of experimental validity.
- Collect only data tied to a defined product or research purpose.
- Separate observed behavior, interpretation, and commercial claim.
- Do not claim predictive capability before out-of-sample validation.

## 6. Product quality

Early does not mean visually disposable. A L T E R . should feel intentional, fluid, distinctive, and trustworthy. It must not resemble a generic white-label commerce interface.

## 7. AI usage

AI is used to accelerate research, specification, design, implementation, review, and analysis. AI output is not accepted without task-appropriate verification.

AI agents operate under bounded assignments and return structured deliverables. Parallelism is used only when it reduces elapsed work without creating contradictory sources of truth.

## 8. Research rule

Research runs beside product development. It affects the next release unless it identifies a critical flaw in the current release.

The objective is not to prove A L T E R . correct. The objective is to reduce uncertainty sufficiently to make the next capital-allocation decision.

## 9. Change rule

A frozen decision may be changed when one of the following occurs:

- new user evidence materially contradicts it;
- implementation reveals a material constraint;
- legal, ethical, privacy, security, or validity risk appears;
- a cheaper path provides equivalent learning;
- the decision no longer serves the active work package.

Changes are recorded after action in the decision log. Discussion does not block execution unless the decision is irreversible or high-risk.

## 10. Current priority

Create and release a professional Learning Release 001 that tests whether users naturally discover products, save them, construct outfits, and return to continue those activities.
